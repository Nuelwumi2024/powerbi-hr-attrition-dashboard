What would it cost a company to lose 413 people? I built a Power BI case study to find out. 📊

Meet Brightwave Commerce, a fictional e-commerce company whose CHRO had to answer one question for the board: "Who is leaving, why, and what is it costing us?"

Here's what the data showed:

🔴 27.5% attrition, and accelerating. Exits rose from 17 in 2021 to 183 in 2025. The company now loses more people than it hires.

💰 $13M estimated replacement cost (about 50% of annual salary per leaver).

🎯 It's concentrated, not company-wide. Customer Support loses 49% of its people. HR loses 5%.

🔍 The root causes:
• Pay and career growth drive over half of all exits
• 61% of leavers had under 3 years' tenure
• Overtime lifts attrition from 23% to 38% (65% in Customer Support)
• Low satisfaction scores signal exits before they happen

✅ My recommendations: benchmark pay for junior frontline roles, cap overtime, build a first-two-years programme, and set up a monthly early-warning list.

What I built: a star-schema model, DAX measures (time intelligence, USERELATIONSHIP, tenure bands, cost modelling) and a 3-page report that goes from KPI to root cause to costed action.

The company and data are fictional and synthetic, but the method applies to any HR team.

Full case study, DAX and the .pbix file: https://github.com/Nuelwumi2024/powerbi-hr-attrition-dashboard

I'd love your feedback. What would you add to an attrition analysis?
